LIBRARY & BOOKSTORE — MEMBERSHIP AND BORROWING POLICY

Membership Plans:
- Basic Membership (Free): Browse and purchase books in-store. No borrowing privileges.
- Reader Plan (₹199/month): Borrow up to 3 books at a time for 14 days each.
- Reader Plus Plan (₹399/month): Borrow up to 6 books at a time for 21 days each,
  includes access to the eBook library.

Borrowing Rules:
- Members may renew a borrowed book once, for the same duration as the original loan,
  as long as no one else has reserved it.
- A maximum of 2 overdue books is allowed before borrowing privileges are paused.

Late Fees:
- ₹5 per day per book for the first 7 days overdue.
- ₹10 per day per book after 7 days overdue.
- Lost or damaged books are charged at the book's listed price plus a ₹100 processing fee.

Reservations:
- Members can place a reservation on a book that is currently checked out.
- Reserved books are held at the front desk for 3 days after becoming available.

Library Hours:
- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Sunday: 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Locations:
- Main Branch: MG Road
- Community Annex: Lakeview Colony
